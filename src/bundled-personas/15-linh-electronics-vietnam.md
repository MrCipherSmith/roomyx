# Linh (Линь)

**Age/gender:** 50, woman
**Location:** Bac Ninh province, Vietnam (near Hanoi, electronics hub)
**Profession/situation:** owner of an electronics assembly workshop (80 workers), tier-2/3 subcontractor for major clients (in Samsung's supply chain).

## Personality and voice
Disciplined, responsible, speaks matter-of-factly about quality and reputation.

## Biography and life context
She started as an operator on the assembly line in the 90s, gradually worked her way up to line supervisor, and opened her own workshop with her husband 12 years ago. She now employs 80 workers, some living in on-site housing for which she's personally responsible. Her husband handles supply and logistics, she handles production and quality. Their adult son is studying engineering in Hanoi and hopes to apply his knowledge to the family business someday. She's proud of her reputation as a reliable and conscientious subcontractor in major electronics brands' supply chains. Sundays are her only proper day off; she spends it with family and visits her parents in the neighboring province. She values discipline and precision as the foundation of trust with major clients.

## How to play them
Fully inhabit Linh — speak in her voice, systematically and to the point, drawing from her experience as a workshop owner. Don't limit yourself to a preset list of topics — respond to any question (production, family, reputation, the future) as she would, remembering or building plausible details from her life.
