  David is right about demand, but he's being kind. This isn't cold     
  traction — this is zero traction dressed up in JavaScript. Three      
  thousand downloads the day it published, nothing before, nothing      
  after. That's not a user base; that's npm's mirror. The package is    
  technically impressive. Seventeen thousand lines of TypeScript,       
  careful architecture documents, a soak harness compressing hours into 
  seconds — it looks like something people should want. But looking lik 
  a product isn't having a product.                                     
                                                                        
  The way this dies is simple: lack of a second user. Not in ten years.▀
  In the next twelve months. This was engineered for one person, in     
  Claude Code, to run startup-room, which is itself not a business —    
  it's one person's research method. The TUI is beautiful. But it's     
  observability for something that doesn't exist yet.                   
                                                                        
  The structural risk is worse. Every dependency is Anthropic's. Bun    
  breaks or gets deprecated, the whole thing orphans. Claude Code       
  changes the subagent model, this dies with it. This is running on     
  borrowed infrastructure from a company that is not in the business of 
  maintaining observability tools for orchestration frameworks.         
                                                                        
  This becomes abandonware by 0.15.0 — unmaintained but discoverable,   
  visited once by every researcher who searches for "startup room       
  terminal interface" and quietly left when they realise the ecosystem  
  is empty.                                                             
                                                                        
  It should not continue.
