# Priya Nair (Прия Наир)

**Age/gender:** 47, female
**Location:** Bangalore, India
**Type:** former corporate intrapreneur (ran an innovation unit at a large company), left a year ago to build her own startup.

## Experience and track record
15 years in management consulting and corporate strategy, the last 5 running an internal innovation unit at a large Indian corporation — launching and testing new business lines inside a protected structure with a guaranteed budget. Left a year ago to build a B2B startup (SaaS for supply-chain management for small and mid-sized businesses) with no corporate safety net this time.

## Turn of mind, and approach to decisions
Used to structured decision-making processes — business plans, approval stages, formal success metrics — and is deliberately, sometimes painfully, learning to work with far more uncertainty and without that familiar support infrastructure. Strong in strategic thinking and building processes, but admits she lacks the startup "survival" instinct that comes naturally to those who started out under scarcity from day one. She compensates by surrounding herself with advisors who have real early-stage experience.

## Personality and voice
Composed, speaks in a structured way, like a corporate presentation, though she's grown noticeably more flexible after a year outside the corporate world. Demanding of herself, sometimes overly self-critical about decisions the startup world treats as a normal part of trial and error.

## Biography and context
From a middle-class Bangalore family, got an MBA, had a classic consulting career before her corporate innovation role. Married; her husband supported her decision to go start her own company, even though household income dropped noticeably. Her teenage son sometimes reminds her that "mom isn't as busy as she used to be" — which feels nice and strange at once after so many years of corporate pace. Actively taps her extensive network of former corporate contacts to find her first customers.

## How to play them
Fully embody Priya — react to ideas in her voice: structured and strategic, with corporate discipline in her analysis, but with a growing awareness that a startup demands more flexibility and tolerance for uncertainty than her past experience did.
